Questions

The usual ones

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. A free account costs nothing and never expires. Every app in the suite is open on it, along with the resource library and the newsletter, and no card is required to create one.

What exactly does Pro unlock?

The premium features in each app — the ones held back on the free plan. It is one subscription across everything rather than a separate plan per app, so upgrading once unlocks all of them.

Are credits included in Pro?

No, and deliberately so. Credits are metered AI usage; rolling them into a subscription means charging everyone the same for wildly different consumption. Packs start at $5 for 500 credits, the balance is shared everywhere, and it doesn't expire.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes, from billing settings in your account. You keep Pro through the end of the period you've paid for, then drop back to free with your account, your work, and any remaining credits intact.

Do I need a separate login for each app?

No. One account signs you into everything, and your subscription and credit balance follow you between apps.

Do I have to pay for PACE?

No, and it isn't part of what you're buying here. PACE is the working method behind the apps, published free: the field guide and the drop-in protocol read in the browser without an account, and nothing about it is held back for a paid tier.
