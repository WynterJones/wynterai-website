Questions

The usual ones

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. A Wynter account costs nothing and never expires. It opens every app in the suite plus the resource library, and no card is required to create one.

What exactly does Pro unlock?

The premium features inside each app — the ones held back on the free plan. It is one subscription across the whole suite rather than a separate plan per product, so upgrading once unlocks all of them.

Are credits included in Pro?

No, and deliberately so. Credits are metered AI usage; rolling them into a subscription means charging everyone the same for wildly different consumption. Packs start at $5 for 500 credits, the balance is shared across every app, and it doesn't expire.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes, from billing settings in your account. You keep Pro through the end of the period you've paid for, then drop back to free with your account, your work, and any remaining credits intact.

Do I need a separate login for each app?

No. One Wynter account signs you into every app in the suite, and your subscription and credit balance follow you between them.
