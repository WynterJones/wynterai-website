Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

This policy explains what Wynter.ai collects, why, who it goes to, and what you can do about it. It covers this website, your Wynter account at account.wynter.ai, and the apps in the Wynter suite.

The controller of your personal data is Wynter.ai, operated by Wynter Jones. Contact: privacy@wynter.ai.

1. What we collect

Account data

Your email address, name, password hash, and any profile details or avatar you add. We never store your password itself.

Content you create

What you put into the apps — projects, prompts, documents, and the output generated from them.

Billing data

Subscription status, credit balance and transactions, and purchase history. Payments are handled by Stripe; card numbers go to Stripe, not to us.

Technical data

IP address, browser and device type, and timestamps, recorded in server and security logs — including the session and connected-app records shown on your security page, which exist so you can spot access you don't recognise.

Marketing preferences

Whether you opted in to product emails, and when. Product emails only go to people who ticked the box.

2. Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To run the Services — creating your account, signing you in, keeping your work, and deciding what your account unlocks. Basis: performance of a contract.
  • To take payment — subscriptions, credit packs, receipts, and refunds. Basis: performance of a contract.
  • To keep accounts secure — detecting abuse, fraud, and unauthorised access, and honouring session revocation. Basis: legitimate interests.
  • To support you — answering the messages you send us. Basis: legitimate interests.
  • To send product emails — only where you opted in. Basis: consent, withdrawable at any time.
  • To meet legal obligations — tax, accounting, and lawful requests. Basis: legal obligation.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your content to train our own models.

3. AI processing

Features that generate output send the input you provide to third-party AI model providers so they can return a result. We use providers under agreements that prohibit training their models on data sent through our API access. Don't put secrets, credentials, or personal data about other people into these features unless you have the right to.

4. Who we share it with

Only with the service providers needed to run the Services, each acting on our instructions:

  • Stripe — payment processing and subscription billing.
  • AI model providers — generating output from your input.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers — running the applications, databases, and this website.
  • Email delivery providers — sending sign-in links, receipts, security alerts, and opted-in product email.
  • Error and performance monitoring — diagnosing faults.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, or to establish or defend legal claims. If the business is ever sold or transferred, account data may transfer with it; you'd be told before that happens.

5. Cookies and analytics

This marketing site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Your account and the apps use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and to protect forms — these can't be switched off without breaking sign-in.

Where we measure traffic, we use privacy-respecting, cookie-free analytics that record aggregate page views without profiling individuals across sites.

6. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where data leaves the UK or EEA, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and content — while your account is open, then deleted or anonymised within 30 days of closure.
  • Billing records — up to 7 years, because tax and accounting law requires it.
  • Security and server logs — typically 90 days.
  • Marketing preferences — including a record of unsubscribes, kept so we can honour the opt-out.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, export it in a portable format, restrict or object to how we use it, or withdraw consent. Product email carries an unsubscribe link in every message, and email preferences are switchable in your account.

Email privacy@wynter.ai and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're in the UK or EEA and you're not happy with the outcome, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit, passwords are hashed, and access to production data is limited to what's needed to operate the Services. Your account's security page lists active sessions and connected apps so you can revoke anything you don't recognise. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we'll notify you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.

10. Children

The Services aren't for children. You must be at least 16 (or the age of digital consent where you live, if higher) to create an account. If we learn we've collected data from a child below that age, we'll delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top always reflects the current version, and we'll give notice by email or in the Services before a material change takes effect.

12. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: privacy@wynter.ai. See also the Terms of Service.
