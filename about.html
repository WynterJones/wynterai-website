About

Wynter Jones

I build the tools I needed and couldn't find — then hand them to everyone else on the same account.

Wynter.ai is a suite of small, sharp products for the work that sits between an idea and a shipped thing: figuring out the offer, planning the build, writing the spec, keeping the tasks moving. Each one started as something I built for my own projects because the alternative was a blank page and a lot of guessing.

They are separate apps on purpose. A single tool that claims to do all of it ends up doing none of it well — so BuildThisFunnel only cares about funnels, CreateThisOffer only cares about offers, and so on. What holds them together isn't a mega-app, it's one account.

One account, on purpose

Every app in the suite runs on a single Wynter account. One login, one subscription, one shared credit balance — not seven separate signups and seven separate invoices. Sign up once and every app is already open when you get there.

That's also why the free plan is genuinely free rather than a trial with a countdown. You should be able to use the suite, get something out of it, and only pay when you hit the part that's worth paying for.

Open source too

Not everything is a product. Farmwork is an open, free workflow framework for agentic development with Claude Code and other AI coding tools — the same harness I use to build everything here. OpenPaw is a self-hosted AI workbench: you describe the tool you need, agents build it as a real running service, and the whole thing stays on your own machine.

Both are free, both are open to read, and both work with the Wynter account you already have.

How I build

Small products, shipped fast, then sharpened. I'd rather put a narrow tool in front of people this month than a broad one next year, and I'd rather fix the thing people actually hit than the thing I imagined they'd hit. Most of what's in the suite exists because a real project of mine needed it first.
