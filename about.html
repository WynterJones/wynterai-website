About

PACE is how I work.

It isn’t a theory I wrote up. It’s the method behind the software on this site, after 25 years of building for the web.

PACE is two laws for working with coding agents: act in parallel, so your attention stops being the thing they wait on, and compound effort, so the tenth task is faster than the first. It's free, and it's the way I actually work.

That distinction is the whole reason this page exists. A framework nobody has run is a guess with a diagram attached. This one came out of shipping — the apps on this site, the harness underneath them, and a lot of ordinary days finding out which parts of it survive a normal working week.

Who's behind it

I'm Wynter Jones, and I've been building for the web for more than 25 years. Recent work includes ClickFunnels Classic and MarketingSecretsAI, and I work alongside a lot of the marketers who set the pace in this space — Russell Brunson among them.

I work small and ship often: narrow tools, in front of people early, sharpened by whatever actually breaks. It's an unglamorous way to build and it's the only one I've seen hold up — a tool nobody has used yet is a guess, however good the plan was.

Where the method shows up

Farmwork is an open, free workflow framework for agentic development with Claude Code and other AI coding tools — the same harness I use to build everything here. It is what the two laws look like once they stop being advice and become software.

OpenPaw is a self-hosted AI workbench: you describe the tool you need, agents build it as a real running service, and the whole thing stays on your own machine. Both are free, both are open to read, and both work with the Wynter account you already have.

Wynter.ai itself is a suite of apps for building with AI — planning the build, writing the spec, shaping the brand, keeping the tasks moving. They are separate apps on purpose: a single tool that claims to do all of it ends up doing none of it well, so BuildThisFunnel only cares about funnels and DoTheseTasks only cares about keeping the work moving. Every one of them started in a real project where I needed the thing, couldn't buy it, and built it anyway — then used it long enough to find out where it was wrong and fixed that.

Why PACE is free

The framework costs nothing and always will. The two laws, the templates and the protocol are on this site in full, and the entire field guide reads in the browser with no account and no email form.

That's the same reason the free tools, the training, and the checklists sit out front rather than behind a wall. The fastest way to learn whether something works is to watch people use it. A method you can only evaluate after paying isn't a method, it's a brochure.

One account, on purpose

Every app runs on a single Wynter account. One login, one subscription, one shared credit balance — not a separate signup and a separate invoice per app. Sign up once and everything is already open when you get there.

That's also why the free plan is genuinely free rather than a trial with a countdown. You should be able to use the tools, get something out of it, and only pay when you hit the part that's worth paying for.
