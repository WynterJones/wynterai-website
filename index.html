03 The method

PACE, free and complete.

The framework costs nothing and always will. Both laws, the field guide and the protocol are published here in full, readable without an account, with nothing held back for a paid tier.

The bottleneck moved

AI didn't 10x you. Here's why.

You got a coding agent that writes a module in four minutes and your week did not get five times better. Your attention is single-threaded, and nothing you learn stays learned. PACE is two laws for exactly those two problems.

  • Law oneAct in Parallel

    Your agents can run concurrently. You can't. So stop being the thing they wait on: give a task enough context to run for an hour, and get every blocking question answered before it starts.

  • Law twoCompound Effort

    If your tenth task isn't faster than your first, you don't have a system — you have a habit. Fix the context that produced a wrong answer, not just the answer.
